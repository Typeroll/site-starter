# Verification

- Confirm Site, Version and deployed/source identity.
- Read back saved content and inspect desktop/mobile previews.
- Exercise menus, keyboard focus, links and enabled app states.
- Verify routes, metadata, media and publishing readiness before launch.
- Check that structured data (FAQ, recipe, event) matches what each page shows.
- Record tested pages, viewports, results and unresolved issues.
- Store screenshots and private runtime reports in ignored reports/.
- Publishing validation does not prove visual or editorial completeness.
