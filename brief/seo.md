# SEO strategy

## Business goals, audience, markets and languages

## Search intent and topic coverage

Record research sources and dates. Distinguish evidence from assumptions; do not invent traffic or ranking forecasts.

## Page roles and URL plan

Content types, useful landing pages, taxonomy, internal links and routes to preserve or redirect. Avoid near-duplicate pages created only for keyword variants.

## Indexing and canonical policy

Which pages should be indexable, public noindex, or private? Record deliberate canonical overrides and sitemap policy; actual values live in the CMS.

## Editorial standards and structured data

Unique useful copy, supported claims, descriptive titles and links, accessible image text, schema matching visible content. No fixed title length or word count is a hard SEO rule.

## Measurement and review

Baseline, analytics/consent decisions, measures of success and review dates. Never store credentials or private analytics exports here.
