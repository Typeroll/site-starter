# Owner preferences and project rules

## Confirmed rules

Record each rule, its scope, who approved it and the date. Separate confirmed rules from proposals.

## Writing and factual accuracy

Voice, terminology, prohibited claims, source requirements and legal copy that must not be rewritten.

## Design and editing

Brand constraints, accessibility, editable content, standard/custom blocks and documented HTML exceptions.

## Operations and approval

Who may approve content, publication, DNS changes and messages? Credentials stay outside this repository.

## Exceptions and superseded rules

Link decisions.md; do not silently override a confirmed rule. Project notes do not grant permissions beyond the user's authorization or account access.
